## What happened after the arb closed

After the persistent MSUSD/USDC arb closed during the index-fix outage at
2026-05-13 ~06:19 UTC, the detector ran cleanly for **another ~2.5 days
(~104K blocks)** and produced **zero JSONL records**. The container was
healthy throughout: sub-second lag, sync cycles every 5 min with 0 errors,
no GAP markers, no Tracebacks.

This is a hard empirical signal:

- The persistent MSUSD/USDC arb has **not reopened** within 3 days
  (resolves UNK-002 negatively)
- **No transient arbs** appeared on the 128-pool monitored set at the
  current floors ($500K UniV3+Slipstream, $250K Aerodrome v1)
- Post-index-fix lag stayed sub-1s for the entire 2.5-day silent window
  (resolves UNK-006 positively)

## Why the run extended past 1 day

The `--minutes 1440` timer was supposed to terminate the run at
2026-05-14 ~05:33 UTC. Container kept running until externally killed
via `railway down` ~2 days later. Either the asyncio cancellation chain
didn't fire on timer expiry, or Railway auto-restarted the container
after a clean exit despite `restartPolicyType=ON_FAILURE`. **Filed as a
failure** — see `failures/FAILURE_LOG.md` 2026-05-16 entry.
