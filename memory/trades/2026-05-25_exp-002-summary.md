### What's driving the flags

All 1,047 hard flags fired rule_2 `org_wallet_membership` (Tier A) on a small set of pool addresses — chiefly `0xc6962004f452be9203591991d15f6b388e09e8d0` (the canonical WETH/USDC Slipstream pool on Arbitrum at fee tier ~5 bps). 2 of those records ALSO triggered rule_3 `drain_detected_approval`. Layer 3's behavioral corpus has classified the contracts running this pool as `org_wallet`-tier, marking the entire arb path as flagged.

**Interpretation note**: 84.5% flag rate on cross-chain is the highest yield by far across the entire EXP-002 dataset. But the flag is concentrated on ONE pool (which appears as src OR dst across most cross-chain routes). It's not "5 distinct opportunities flagged" — it's "9 of 12 unique routes touched a single L3-flagged pool". The H2 letter ("Layer 3 flags ≥1% of detected opportunities") is met decisively; the H3 spirit (do flagged vs unflagged differ structurally?) needs n>1 flagged route to test.
